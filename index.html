About

A contest for university students, run like the regionals

ICPC Hungary is the Hungarian national contest of the International Collegiate Programming Contest — the oldest and largest algorithmic programming competition for university students in the world. It is organised by the ELTE Faculty of Informatics.

  • Teams of threeStudents from the same university compete together.
  • One computerAll three members share a single workstation.
  • 12–13 problemsAlgorithmic tasks of increasing difficulty, in English.
  • About five hoursSolve as many problems as possible before time runs out.
  • ICPC scoringMost problems solved wins; ties are broken by total penalty time.
  • Live scoreboardStandings update during the contest, frozen near the end.

Rules and participation

ICPC Hungary follows the official ICPC Regional rules. The same rules govern eligibility, team composition and official participation, so anything that applies at a regional contest applies here too.

Read the ICPC Regional rules

Advancing to CERC

The top teams from each university advance to the Central European Regional Contest (CERC), where they compete against the strongest teams from the wider region.

Venues

Two contest sites, one contest

ICPC Hungary is organised by the ELTE Faculty of Informatics and runs simultaneously at two locations, with a single combined scoreboard.

Budapest

Eötvös Loránd University

Faculty of Informatics, host and organiser of the contest.

Szeged

University of Szeged

Second contest site, hosting teams from Szeged and the surrounding region.
